Features

Everything you need in one place

Built for students, maintenance staff, and administrators to work together seamlessly.

AI Priority Classification

Our machine learning model analyses every request and automatically assigns a High, Medium or Low priority — so urgent issues like electrical faults or flooding are never buried in a backlog.

Real-time Status Tracking

Students see their request status update live — from Pending to In Progress to Resolved — without refreshing the page. You always know exactly where your issue stands.

Photo Uploads

Attach a photo of the damage when submitting a request. A picture helps staff understand the problem immediately and arrive prepared with the right tools and materials.

Staff Assignment

Administrators can assign each request to the right maintenance staff member, add notes, and override the AI priority when needed — keeping the workflow efficient and accountable.

Predictive Analytics

The analytics dashboard reveals which hostels, wings and issue types are generating the most requests, enabling proactive preventive maintenance before small problems become big ones.

Instant Notifications

Everyone stays informed with in-app notifications at every step — request received, staff assigned, status changed, and resolved — so nothing falls through the cracks.

How it works

From report to resolution in three steps

A simple process designed so issues get fixed quickly and transparently.

1

Submit a request

Describe the issue, attach a photo, and specify your hostel, wing and room number. The whole form takes under two minutes.

2

AI prioritises & assigns

The AI model classifies urgency instantly. An admin reviews and assigns the request to the right maintenance staff member.

3

Track to resolution

Follow your request live and receive notifications at every status change. Once resolved, the resolution time is recorded for reporting.
